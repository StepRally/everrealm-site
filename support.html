01 · Getting back in

Sign-in help

Choose Sign In on the welcome screen and use the email address and password for your EverRealm account. Check the email spelling and your internet connection.

If you have forgotten your password, choose Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and follow the reset instructions. Check your spam or junk folder for the email. If an email does not arrive, avoid sending repeated requests and contact support.

For support without signing in, choose Begin Your Journey on the welcome screen, then Support and safety on the registration screen. You do not need to create an account to open that help screen.

02 · Something isn’t right

Report a message or shared content

To report a received chat message, open your conversation. From the safety screen, you can choose Pause chat or report a received message. Press and hold a message sent by the other person, choose Report in the confirmation, and look for Report received.

Other eligible content has its own report control: Report memory in another person’s memory details, Report invitation on a received invitation, and Report shared profile in the connection panel. Mutually revealed adventure writing has a control beside the shared text, such as Report revealed answer or Report revealed whisper.

For these shared-content reports, choose a safety reason and then Send report. Look for Report received for safety review. If the result is not confirmed, use Retry report; do not assume the report was saved.

Only the selected shared content and safety reason are submitted; private drafts, the rest of your conversation and unrelated writing are not copied. A memory report includes its caption and reference, not an extra copy of the photo. Reporting needs no approval from the other person and does not give them access to your report.

Reporting does not automatically pause chat, block contact or disconnect you. Use those controls separately if needed. You can also email about a safety concern that is not covered by an available report control. No review or response time is promised.

03 · Stop new contact

Block contact and shared activity

Open Safety, support and your account and find Block contact and shared activity. For the relevant contact, choose Block this person, read the explanation and confirm with Block person.

Blocking stops new chat, invitations, connection requests and shared adventure actions between you in both directions. Unlike chat pause, it is not limited to messages. Blocking does not disconnect you, erase permitted history or prevent either person from reporting, deleting or withdrawing supported permissions. Disconnect and account deletion remain separate.

Only you can remove your own block using Remove my block. Doing so does not remove the other person’s block, restore cancelled connection requests or grant new consent.

If a decision is not confirmed, use Retry the same block decision. Use Refresh blocked contacts to reload the current state rather than assuming a block or unblock completed.

04 · Take a little space

Pause chat without disconnecting

In your conversation, open Chat safety using the shield control, then choose Pause messages.

Pausing stops new messages in both directions. It does not disconnect the pair, erase existing messages or remove all access to shared features and history. You do not need the other person’s approval. Use contact blocking instead if you also want to stop new invitations, connection requests and shared adventure actions.

To undo your own pause, return to Chat safety and choose Allow messages again. You cannot undo a pause set by the other person. Allowing messages again does not remove a separate contact block.

05 · Before a photo is shared

Pending photo review and finishing a save

New personal-photo and mixed-content image uploads wait privately for human safety review. In Memories, the uploader can check Photo review and choose Refresh photo review. An upload marked Awaiting safety review — not shared is not available for the other person to view.

Approval alone does not share the upload. After approval, the original uploader must choose the item’s Finish saving button, which includes a short upload identifier. Saving rechecks the original permissions, consent and current connection restrictions; approval does not override a block, disconnect or changed permission.

If the result is uncertain, follow the displayed error and use Retry the same upload action where offered. Do not create a replacement to get around a failed or unconfirmed save. The uploader can use the item’s Delete upload control independently. A rejected upload is not shared; retrying does not make a replacement or approve it.

The review list shows the most recent 50 submitted uploads. An item missing from that list is not, by itself, proof of deletion or sharing. No review turnaround is promised, and existing shared photos are unchanged by this new-upload review step.

Not every keepsake requires personal-photo review. The separate Story-only keepsake — no personal content option uses fixed story content, not personal photos or user writing. Personal-photo and mixed-content consent and review rules still apply to their own save options.

06 · End a connection

Disconnect from your paired person

On Safety, support and your account, choose Disconnect connection, then review the explanation and choose Disconnect to confirm. This control appears when you have a current connection.

Disconnecting ends current membership for both people without requiring the other person’s approval. It does not delete either account or erase all shared history. Permitted history remains limited to the original people; a future connection cannot see it.

If the result is uncertain, use Refresh connection status rather than assuming the disconnect completed.

07 · A permanent choice

Delete your account

  1. Open Delete Account from the safety screen, or Account deletion on the welcome screen. Sign in to select your own account if needed.
  2. Read what will be removed and what stays with the other person. Type DELETE, then choose Review deletion.
  3. Only if you want to proceed, choose Permanently delete account in the final confirmation.

Deletion removes your sign-in account, profile, preferences, uploaded memories and your own activity contributions. Uploaded photo files are queued for permanent removal. The other person’s account and independently owned data are not deleted, and shared history is not erased as a whole.

This cannot be undone. Signing out is different: it keeps your account. Follow any error or retry instructions shown; deletion and file cleanup are not promised to be immediate.

08 · Your choices

Manage sharing permissions

Choose Manage sharing permissions from the notice confirmation screen or Safety, support and your account. Sign in to see controls for your account. You do not have to accept updated notices to open these controls.

The screen only withdraws supported quotation permissions or removes saved memories where you already have that right. It cannot share writing, grant consent, advance an adventure, reverse a past writing reveal or recall screenshots and other external copies. An empty permission list does not mean all past sharing has been revoked.

Withdraw quotation permission is offered for A Promise Remembered, Wilds, Kingdom and The Midnight Letter only where the current adventure rules allow it, before a keepsake is saved. Withdrawal may invalidate an unfinished keepsake reservation. A completed keepsake must use its existing removal rules; withdrawal does not authorize a replacement.

Paris responses and reflections use mutual-answer reveal rules, not a reversible reveal switch. Legacy Manor whispers have no separate withdrawal switch. The Last Alibi has no separate quotation-consent control here; this screen does not remove evidence, decisions or investigation progress. Locked or expired adventures are not unlocked by these controls.

Uploaders can use Remove saved memory for their saved memories. The Midnight Letter also allows an eligible quoted author to remove its whole saved keepsake. That author-removal right does not apply to every adventure. Review the confirmation and choose Remove saved memory only if you want to proceed. Removal cannot be undone here, does not reset writing and does not permit replacing a completed keepsake. Use Retry saved memory removal if offered after an interrupted removal.

Reporting, contact blocking, chat pause, disconnect and account deletion are also available without accepting updated notices. Account-specific actions still require sign-in. Blocking and quotation withdrawal are separate decisions; neither should be treated as proof that the other has happened. For a control not offered, contact support, without assuming a guaranteed removal procedure or response deadline.

09 · Get in touch

Contact safely

Email everrealmapp@gmail.com. EverRealm is created and operated by Elaina Berry.

Describe the screen you were on, the steps you took, the error text and whether you use iPhone or Android. Include your app version if you know it, but avoid sharing another person’s private messages.

Never email passwords, sign-in codes, identity documents or sensitive images. If your email app does not open, copy the address above into your preferred email service.

Support is not continuously monitored, and no response time is guaranteed. EverRealm is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
