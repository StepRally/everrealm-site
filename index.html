Follow a story together

Share choices, explore authored adventures and find a reason to pause your day for each other. The people you encounter in a story are fictional characters.

Stay connected

Bring a little intention to your conversations, whether you are across the room or far apart. EverRealm is made for the person you choose: a romantic partner or a best friend.

Keep meaningful moments

Save photos you choose and revisit memories available to your connection. Writing reveals and keepsake quotations follow each adventure’s own sharing rules.
